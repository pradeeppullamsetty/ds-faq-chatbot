# Sample College - Admissions Information

## Admission Process Overview
Sample College follows a transparent and merit-based admission process for all undergraduate and postgraduate programs. The academic year begins in July, with applications opening in March and closing by end of May.

## Undergraduate Admissions (B.Tech, B.Sc, BBA, BCA)

### Eligibility Criteria
- **B.Tech Programs**: Candidates must have passed 10+2 or equivalent examination with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as compulsory subjects. Minimum 60% aggregate marks (55% for reserved categories).
- **B.Sc Programs**: 10+2 with Science stream (PCM/PCB) with minimum 55% aggregate marks.
- **BBA/BCA Programs**: 10+2 in any stream with minimum 50% aggregate marks.

### Entrance Exams Accepted
- **JEE Main** (for B.Tech): Valid score required. College cutoff varies by branch (typically 85th percentile and above).
- **State CET** (for B.Tech/B.Sc): Valid rank in state common entrance test.
- **College Entrance Test (CET-SC)**: Conducted by Sample College for direct admission to B.Sc, BBA, and BCA programs. Exam held in May.

### Application Process
1. **Online Registration**: Visit admissions.samplecollege.edu.in and create an account
2. **Fill Application Form**: Personal details, academic qualifications, program preferences
3. **Upload Documents**:
   - 10th and 12th mark sheets
   - Entrance exam scorecard
   - Category certificate (if applicable)
   - Passport-size photograph
   - Aadhar card
4. **Pay Application Fee**: INR 1,500 (General), INR 1,000 (Reserved categories)
5. **Submit and Track**: Receive application ID for status tracking

### Important Dates (Tentative for 2024-25)
- Application Opens: March 1, 2024
- Application Closes: May 31, 2024
- CET-SC Exam: June 15, 2024
- Results Declaration: June 30, 2024
- Counseling Starts: July 10, 2024
- Classes Commence: August 1, 2024

## Postgraduate Admissions (M.Tech, M.Sc, MBA, MCA)

### Eligibility Criteria
- **M.Tech**: B.Tech/B.E. in relevant branch with minimum 60% aggregate (55% reserved). Valid GATE score preferred.
- **M.Sc**: B.Sc in relevant subject with minimum 55% aggregate.
- **MBA**: Bachelor's degree in any discipline with minimum 50% aggregate. CAT/MAT/XAT/CMAT score accepted.
- **MCA**: BCA/B.Sc (CS/IT) with minimum 55% aggregate. Valid NIMCET or state MCA entrance score.

### Application Process
Similar to UG process, application fee: INR 2,000 (General), INR 1,500 (Reserved).

## Lateral Entry Admissions
- **B.Tech Lateral Entry**: Diploma holders in Engineering (3-year) with minimum 60% marks. Admission to 2nd year (3rd semester).
- **MCA Lateral Entry**: BCA/B.Sc (CS) graduates admitted to 2nd year.

## International Students
- Separate quota of 15% seats in each program
- Eligibility: Equivalent qualifications recognized by AIU
- Application through ICCR or direct with additional documents (passport, visa, equivalence certificate)

## Reservation Policy
As per Government of India norms:
- SC: 15%
- ST: 7.5%
- OBC-NCL: 27%
- EWS: 10%
- PwD: 5% horizontal reservation

## Documents Required at Admission
Originals + 2 sets of self-attested photocopies:
1. All mark sheets and certificates (10th onwards)
2. Entrance exam scorecard
3. Transfer/Migration Certificate
4. Character Certificate
5. Category Certificate (if applicable)
6. Medical Fitness Certificate
7. Anti-ragging affidavit (student + parent)
8. 6 passport-size photographs
9. Aadhar card copy
