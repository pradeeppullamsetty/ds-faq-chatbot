## Placement Process

### Pre-Placement Preparation (Starts 3rd Year)
1. **Aptitude Training**: Quantitative, Logical, Verbal (100+ hours)
2. **Technical Training**: DSA, System Design, Domain-specific (120+ hours)
3. **Coding Platforms**: LeetCode, CodeChef, HackerRank premium access
4. **Mock Interviews**:
   - Technical: By alumni/industry experts (4 rounds/student)
   - HR: By corporate trainers (2 rounds/student)
   - Group Discussion: Weekly sessions
5. **Resume Building**: Workshops, reviews, ATS optimization
6. **Soft Skills**: Communication, Presentation, Leadership, Email etiquette

### Placement Season Timeline
| Period | Activity |
|--------|----------|
| **May-Jun (Pre-final yr)** | Registration, Profile building, Skill assessment |
| **Jul-Aug** | Intensive training, Mock drives, Company webinars |
| **Sep-Nov** | **Main Placement Season** - Core companies, Dream companies |
| **Dec-Jan** | **Secondary Season** - Mass recruiters, Remaining eligible |
| **Feb-Mar** | **Final Phase** - Off-campus referrals, Pool drives |
| **Apr-Jun** | Extended support for unplaced, Higher studies guidance |

### Eligibility Criteria (Company-specific, typical minimums)
- **CGPA**: 6.5-7.0 (varies by company)
- **Active Backlogs**: Zero (most companies)
- **Attendance**: Minimum 75%
- **Branch**: As per job description
- **Internship**: Preferred (mandatory for some)

### Placement Policy Highlights
- **One Student - One Offer** (until all eligible get at least one)
- **Dream Company Policy**: Can apply to dream companies even after getting offer
- **Core Company Policy**: Core branch students get priority for core companies
- **Debarment**:
  - Rejecting offer after acceptance: Debarred for season
  - False information: Debarred + disciplinary action
  - No-show for interview: Warning, then debarment
