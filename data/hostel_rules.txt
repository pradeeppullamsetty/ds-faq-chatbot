# Sample College - Hostel Facilities and Rules

## Hostel Overview
Sample College provides on-campus hostel accommodation for approximately 3,000 students across 8 hostel blocks. Separate blocks for boys and girls with dedicated wardens and security.

## Room Allotment Policy
- **Priority**: PG students > Final year UG > Senior years > First year
- **Merit Basis**: Within same category, higher CGPA gets preference
- **Application**: Online through student portal (opens June 15)
- **Allotment**: First week of July (before classes start)
- **Waiting List**: Maintained throughout the year

## Mess Facilities
- **Two mess halls per hostel block** (veg/non-veg separate counters)
- **Menu**: North Indian, South Indian, Chinese, Continental - weekly rotation
- **Timings**:
  - Breakfast: 7:30 AM - 9:30 AM
  - Lunch: 12:30 PM - 2:30 PM
  - Snacks: 5:00 PM - 6:30 PM
  - Dinner: 7:30 PM - 9:30 PM
- **Special Diets**: Jain, Vegan, Medical diets on request (submit to mess manager)
- **Mess Card**: Biometric/QR based, prepaid wallet system
- **Guest Meals**: Available at nominal charge (prior notice required)

## Hostel Rules and Regulations

### 1. Entry and Exit Timings
| Category | Weekdays | Weekends/Holidays |
|----------|----------|-------------------|
| **Boys Hostels** | 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM | 6:00 AM - 11:00 PM |
| **Girls Hostels** | 6:00 AM - 9:00 PM | 6:00 AM - 10:00 PM |
| **Late Entry** | Up to 11:30 PM (with warden permission) | Up to 12:00 AM (with warden permission) |
| **Emergency Late Entry** | Anytime (security guard + warden notification) | Anytime |

- **Late Entry Register**: Must sign at security gate after permitted hours
- **Night Out Permission**: Maximum 2 nights/month (parent consent required)
- **Vacation Stay**: Allowed with prior written application (INR 200/day)

### 2. Attendance and Roll Call
- **Daily Roll Call**: 9:30 PM (Boys), 9:00 PM (Girls) - mandatory
- **Absent without permission**: Fine INR 100 per instance
- **Proxy attendance**: Strictly prohibited, leads to disciplinary action

### 3. Room Rules
- **Occupancy**: Only allotted students allowed. No unauthorized guests overnight
- **Furniture**: College-provided (bed, study table, chair, cupboard). Damage = recovery cost
- **Electrical Appliances**:
  - Allowed: Laptop, phone charger, table lamp, hair dryer (max 1000W)
  - **Prohibited**: Induction stove, heater, iron, refrigerator, AC (unless provided), microwave
- **Room Lock**: Must lock when leaving. College not responsible for theft
- **Cleanliness**: Daily cleaning by student. Weekly inspection by warden
- **Decorations**: No nails/screws on walls. Use removable hooks only

### 4. Visitor Policy
- **Visiting Hours**:
  - Weekdays: 4:00 PM - 7:00 PM
  - Weekends: 10:00 AM - 7:00 PM
- **Visitor Register**: Mandatory entry at security gate with ID proof
- **Girls Hostel**: Male visitors only in common room (parents/guardians to rooms)
- **Boys Hostel**: Female visitors only in common room
- **Overnight Guests**: Not permitted under any circumstances

### 5. Discipline and Conduct
- **Ragging**: Zero tolerance. Immediate expulsion + police complaint (UGC Regulations 2009)
- **Alcohol/Drugs/Smoking**: Strictly prohibited. Expulsion + legal action
- **Noise**: Silence hours 10:00 PM - 7:00 AM. No loud music/speakers
- **Common Areas**: TV room, reading room, gym - maintain decorum
- **Corridor/balcony**: No drying clothes, no loitering after 10 PM

### 6. Leave and Absence
- **Local Guardian**: Must be registered (within 50km) for emergency contact
- **Short Leave (same day)**: Application to warden 2 hours prior
- **Long Leave (overnight)**: Parent/guardian written request + local guardian confirmation
- **Medical Leave**: Medical certificate from college doctor/hospital required
- **Unauthorized Absence > 3 days**: Parents informed, disciplinary action

### 7. Maintenance and Complaints
- **Complaint Register**: At each block office / online portal
- **Response Time**:
  - Electrical/Plumbing: 24 hours
  - Carpentry/Masonry: 48 hours
  - AC/Water heater: 72 hours (outsourced)
- **Emergency**: Call warden/security directly (24x7 numbers displayed)

### 8. Vacation and Re-admission
- **Summer Vacation**: Hostels close (except PG research scholars with permission)
- **Winter Break**: Limited rooms available (apply by Nov 30)
- **Re-admission**: Automatic for continuing students (fee payment by June 30)
- **Vacating Room**: Must vacate within 24 hours of last exam. Late = INR 500/day

## Facilities Available
- **WiFi**: 24x7 high-speed (100 Mbps per block)
- **Laundry**: Coin-operated machines (INR 30/load)
- **Gym**: Basic equipment in each block (6 AM - 10 PM)
- **Common Room**: TV, indoor games (TT, carrom, chess)
- **Reading Room**: 24x7 with AC and charging points
- **Solar Water Heaters**: In all blocks
- **RO Water Purifiers**: On every floor
- **Medical Room**: First aid + visiting doctor (Mon/Wed/Fri 4-6 PM)
- **Ambulance**: 24x7 on campus
