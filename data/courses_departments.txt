# Sample College - Courses and Departments

## Academic Structure
Sample College comprises 8 academic departments offering 22 programs across Engineering, Sciences, Management, and Computer Applications. All programs are approved by AICTE/UGC and affiliated with Sample University.

## Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

### Programs Offered
1. **B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering** (4 Years, 180 Seats)
   - Specializations (from 3rd year): AI & ML, Data Science, Cybersecurity, Cloud Computing, Full Stack Development
   - Curriculum Highlights:
     - Core: Programming, Data Structures, Algorithms, OS, DBMS, CN, SE, Compiler Design
     - Electives: Deep Learning, Blockchain, IoT, Quantum Computing, AR/VR
     - Industry Projects: 2 major projects (6th & 8th semester)
     - Internships: Mandatory 2-month summer internship after 6th semester

2. **B.Tech CSE (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning)** (4 Years, 60 Seats)
   - Focused curriculum on AI/ML from 1st year
   - Specialized labs: NLP, Computer Vision, Robotics
   - Industry partners: NVIDIA, Google Research, Microsoft Research

3. **M.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering** (2 Years, 30 Seats)
   - Specializations: AI, Data Science, Cybersecurity, Software Engineering
   - Research-oriented with thesis in 2nd year
   - GATE qualified preferred

4. **Ph.D. in Computer Science** (Full-time/Part-time)
   - Research areas: ML, NLP, Computer Vision, Security, Distributed Systems

### Key Facilities
- **High-Performance Computing Lab**: 50 GPU workstations (NVIDIA RTX A6000)
- **AI Research Lab**: Dedicated server cluster for deep learning
- **Cybersecurity Range**: Isolated network for ethical hacking practice
- **IoT & Embedded Systems Lab**: Raspberry Pi, Arduino, sensor kits
- **Industry Collaboration**: Microsoft AI Lab, AWS Educate, Google Cloud Credits

### Faculty Strength
- Professors: 8
- Associate Professors: 12
- Assistant Professors: 25
- **Total**: 45 (PhD: 38, Pursuing: 7)

## Credit System and Grading
- **Choice Based Credit System (CBCS)** implemented
- **Semester Credits**: 20-24 (UG), 18-22 (PG)
- **Total Credits for Degree**:
  - B.Tech: 160-170
  - B.Sc/BBA/BCA: 120-130
  - M.Tech: 80-88
  - MBA/MCA: 100-110
- **Grading**: 10-point CGPA (O=10, A+=9, A=8, B+=7, B=6, C=5, P=4, F=0)
- **Passing**: Minimum 4.0 CGPA, no F grades
