## Payment Schedule
- **Odd Semester (July-Dec)**: Due by July 15
- **Even Semester (Jan-Jun)**: Due by January 15
- Late fee: INR 500 per week after due date
- Fee can be paid in 2 installments per semester (with INR 1,000 processing fee)

## Payment Modes
- Online: Net Banking, UPI, Credit/Debit Card via college portal
- Offline: Demand Draft in favor of "Sample College" payable at City
- NEFT/RTGS to college bank account (details on portal)

## Scholarships and Financial Aid

### 1. Merit-Based Scholarships (Automatic - No Separate Application)

| Scholarship | Eligibility | Benefit | Duration |
|-------------|-------------|---------|----------|
| **Chancellor's Excellence** | JEE 95%ile+ / Board 95%+ | 100% Tuition Fee Waiver | Full Program |
| **Dean's Merit** | JEE 90-95%ile / Board 90-95% | 50% Tuition Fee Waiver | Full Program* |
| **Merit Scholarship** | JEE 85-90%ile / Board 85-90% | 25% Tuition Fee Waiver | Full Program* |
| *Subject to maintaining 8.0 CGPA each semester |

### 2. Means-Cum-Merit Scholarships
- **Family Income < INR 2.5 Lakh/year**: 50% Tuition Fee Waiver
- **Family Income INR 2.5-5 Lakh/year**: 25% Tuition Fee Waiver
- Requires income certificate, valid for one year, renewable

### 3. Category-Based Scholarships (Government)
- **Post-Matric Scholarship (SC/ST/OBC)**: As per state government norms
- **EWS Scholarship**: Full tuition fee reimbursement (income < INR 8 Lakh)
- **Minority Scholarship**: As per central government schemes
- **PwD Scholarship**: Fee waiver + assistive devices allowance

### 4. Special Scholarships
- **Sports Excellence**: National/State level players - up to 100% fee waiver
- **Cultural Excellence**: National level achievers - up to 50% fee waiver
- **Single Girl Child**: 25% tuition fee waiver (limited seats)
- **Defense Personnel Wards**: 25% tuition fee waiver
- **COVID-Affected Families**: Special one-time grant (case by case)

### 5. Corporate/Industry Sponsored
- **TCS NQT Toppers**: Full scholarship for CSE/IT students
- **Infosys Foundation**: Merit-cum-means for economically weaker
- **HDFC Educational Crisis**: Emergency support for fee payment

## Scholarship Application Process
1. **Merit Scholarships**: Automatic based on admission scores
2. **Means-Cum-Merit**: Apply online by July 31 with income certificate
3. **Government Scholarships**: Apply through National Scholarship Portal (NSP)
4. **Special Scholarships**: Separate application to Dean Student Welfare by August 15

## Important Notes
- Scholarships are credited as fee adjustment in subsequent semester
- Students must maintain minimum 75% attendance and 6.5 CGPA
- False information leads to cancellation and recovery of amount
- Scholarship amount cannot exceed actual tuition fee

## Fee Refund Policy (As per UGC/AICTE Norms)
| Withdrawal Timeline | Refund Percentage |
|---------------------|-------------------|
| Before classes start | 100% (minus INR 1,000 processing) |
